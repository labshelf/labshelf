"Attention Is All You Need" is a seminal 2017 machine learning research paper by Google researchers that introduced the Transformer architecture.
Key Details
  • Publication Date: June 12, 2017
  • Authors: Ashish Vaswani, Noam Shazeer, Niki Parmar, Jakob Uszkoreit, Llion Jones, and co-authors
  • Core Innovation: Replaced traditional recurrent neural networks (RNNs) and convolutional neural networks (CNNs) with a model relying solely on self-attention mechanisms.
  • Main Advantages:
    • Parallelization:
      Processes all words or tokens in a sequence simultaneously instead of one-by-one.
    • Efficiency: Drastically reduces training time while handling long-range dependencies much better than older sequential models.
  • Impact: The Transformer became the foundational backbone for modern artificial intelligence systems, including Large Language Models (LLMs) like GPT and ChatGPT.
