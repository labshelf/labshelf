# Word frequency lists

`en_50k.txt` and `pt_br_50k.txt` are the 2018 OpenSubtitles lists of FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave,
https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords, commit 525f9b560de45753a5ea01069454e72e9aa541c6
(`content/2018/<code>/<code>_50k.txt`), unchanged. The lists are licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license (CC-BY-SA-4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/.
They are built from OpenSubtitles (http://www.opensubtitles.org/).

LabShelf downloads the lists of other languages from the same commit through jsDelivr when their dictionary is installed.
