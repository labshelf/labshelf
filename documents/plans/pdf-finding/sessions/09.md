## S29 — Closed checklist (A7)

- **Do:**
  - Load the extension from a build of the current branch (`pnpm --filter @labshelf/browser build`) into Chrome and into Firefox, signed in through the institution. If the institution has a library proxy, add it under "Library proxies" in the options.
  - For each of the 15 DOIs in `packages/core/tools/pdf-eval/closed.json`, capture once from the toolbar popup and once from the Google Scholar result. Delete the paper between runs.
  - Count correct PDFs per browser and per method. The pass bar is 12 from the popup and 10 from Scholar.
  - For a side-by-side comparison, build `72b3dc0` (the state before this plan) in a separate worktree and repeat.
- **Context:** Total Cookie Protection in Firefox may keep publisher cookies out of background requests (Q1); a lower Firefox count is a finding, not a failure of the code. A miss that shows "Open to sign in" belongs to S30. R4: Node results do not predict these numbers.
- **Done when:** counts per browser and method are in the log; every miss has its outcome (`bot`, `login`, `html`, `error`) noted from the dialog.

## S30 — Wall dialog (A8)

- **Do:** pick a paper from the S29 misses with a `login` or `bot` outcome. The dialog must offer "Open to sign in" or "Open to pass the check". Click it, sign in or solve the check in the opened tab, and wait without clicking anything else. The toast says the PDF attached, and the paper has its PDF. The watch lasts 5 minutes and survives the service worker sleeping (`storage.session`).
- **Context:** the watcher attaches either the PDF the tab shows or up to 3 PDF links of a page whose `citation_title` is the same work (R3). Not checked before: a real CAFe/Shibboleth sign-in, and Firefox.
- **Done when:** A8 in both browsers, or the failing case is logged with the URL and the tab state.

## S31 — Attach what was downloaded (A11)

- **Do:** with a library paper that has no PDF, download its PDF from any site, then open another PDF in a tab. The toolbar badge shows "PDF" and the popup offers "Attach to <paper>"; one click writes `paper.pdf`. A PDF of an unrelated paper offers nothing.
- **Context:** the offer re-fetches the bytes from the download's final URL with cookies; one-time signed URLs fail (R6), and the popup then offers the id match only. Verified before only in headless Chrome with an arXiv PDF tab. Not checked: Firefox, a real `downloads.onChanged` event.
- **Done when:** A11 in both browsers.

## S32 — Preprint version (A12)

- **Do:** capture a paper whose only free copy is on arXiv. The file attaches with `pdfVersion: preprint` and a "Preprint" chip shows in the browser library, the detail pane and the VS Code library. Run "Find Published Version" on it: the dialog asks first, and warns when highlights exist.
- **Context:** the replace runs through `attachPdfToPaper(..., { replace: true })`. Not checked: the VS Code webview (same core code), a real replace run.
- **Done when:** A12.

## S33 and S34 — Ask the author (A13, A14)

- **Do:**
  - Capture a paper whose page shows an author e-mail (PLOS, Springer or MDPI) with no PDF reachable. The dialog offers "Ask the author"; the click opens the mail client with subject `Request for a copy of "<title>"`, citation, DOI link, and the requester's name and affiliation (set in the options). The detail pane then reads "PDF requested from the author on <date>".
  - In VS Code, open that paper: the same line shows.
  - With no address on the page, the article page opens and nothing is recorded.
- **Context:** the mail client hand-off navigates the captured tab to the `mailto:` (tagged `PLATFORM:` in `askAuthor.ts`); it is unchecked in Chrome and Firefox. The cap is 1,800 characters; the background sends to at most 3 addresses.
- **Done when:** A14 in both browsers, then A13.
