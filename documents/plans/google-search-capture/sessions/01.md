  - Add the repository hosts: PubMed, PMC, Europe PMC, arXiv, bioRxiv, medRxiv, SciELO, Semantic Scholar, OpenAlex and doi.org (D2).

- **Done when:** the host tests pass.

  - Move the split button, its states and its menus out of `content/scholar.ts` into `content/resultButton/`, with no behaviour change.
  - Move `scholar.ts` and `scholarParse.ts` into `content/scholar/`.
  - Rename `ScholarHit` to `SearchHit` and `scholarCapture.ts` to `searchHitCapture.ts`, and update every importer and message name.

D4: the button sits on the result's URL line, in a shadow root.
- **Test first:** n/a, a move; the Scholar tests stay as they are, with renames only.
- **Done when:** A4, and the Scholar tests pass with only renames.

## S1.3 — Google adapter

- **Do:**
  - Save 3 Google result pages as fixtures: a DOI search, a title search, and a `hl=pt-BR` page.
  - Parse organic results into `SearchHit`s, using `extractDoi`, `arxivIdFromUrl` and `pmidFromUrl` from core on the link and snippet, and the "author · year" line for authors and year (Google prints "de TF Smith · 1981").
  - Mount the button only where D2 and D5 allow (organic results on the "All" tab; no ads, AI overview, "People also ask", news, images or video), and watch for results Google adds after load.
  - Add `https://www.google.*/search*` to the content script's `include_globs` in both manifests (D1).
- **Touch:** `packages/browser/src/content/google/`, `packages/browser/manifest.chrome.json`, `manifest.firefox.json`, the build entry. **Do not touch:** `content/resultButton/` beyond its public API.
- **Context:** organic results sit in `div#search` result blocks; key the parser on the result link and its `h3` (R1). A non-article page on a publisher host must end in the existing "not a paper" message (R2).
- **Test first:** Google hit parsing from the 3 fixtures and the ad and AI-overview exclusions (A5).
- **Done when:** A3 and A5.
