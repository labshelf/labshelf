Does self-consistency still help?

Ana Souza, Bruno Lima and Maya Chen

Introduction

A single greedy chain of thought can reach the wrong answer. Sampling several reasoning paths and voting is a known remedy, but every path costs tokens.

Methods

We prompt PaLM-540B with chain-of-thought exemplars, sample diverse reasoning paths and take the majority answer.

Results

On GSM8K accuracy rises from 56.5% to 74.4%, on AQuA from 35.8% to 48.3% and on SVAMP from 79.0% to 86.6%.

Letting a model vote over several reasoning paths beats trusting a single one.
